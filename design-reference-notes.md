# JOJOKEV visual reference notes

The user-provided references show a mobile-first marketplace interface inspired by Jumia: a compact top row with hamburger menu, wordmark, account icon and cart icon; a large rounded search field beneath; category navigation with simple line icons; an account drawer with orders, wishlist and categories; and a clean white canvas with strong dark typography.

The image search used for visual benchmarking returned these reference titles: “TEMU Case Study: Improving User Interface of the TEMU App” (Medium), “Jumia • Case Study by Pixelmatters”, “JUMIA - Sales Analytics Dashboard” (Dribbble), “Understanding And Navigating The Jumia Dashboard” (YouTube), “jumia nigeria website” (Invoice Blog), and “Case study: Let The User Decide a Jumia Checkout Re-design” (Medium).

Design decisions for JOJOKEV: keep the marketplace identity original with deep forest green, lime, warm orange and charcoal; borrow interaction patterns rather than brand assets; make the buyer experience white and discovery-led; keep seller/admin sessions in separate operational shells; route shopping to customer registration and selling to business registration; use the existing public product-images Supabase bucket for seller uploads.
